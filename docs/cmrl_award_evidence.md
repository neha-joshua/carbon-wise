# Chennai Metro Rail Limited award evidence

## What this register contains

`data/evidence/cmrl_awarded_tenders.csv` records 25 Chennai Metro Rail Limited (CMRL) tender or consultancy awards from CMRL's [official awarded-tenders listing](https://chennaimetrorail.org/awarded-tenders/). Each row preserves the tender reference, a concise work-title summary, agency, location wording, notice and award dates as displayed, contractor, award-value text, and stated tax basis.

The rows are procurement evidence records. They are not 25 independently verified completed projects. Some refer to design, feasibility, audit, or DPR services; others describe construction, equipment, systems, or landscaping packages. Different packages may belong to the same larger metro programme. Do not sum the contract values into a project budget or count each package as a separate completed project.

## What the source does and does not establish

- **Supported by the page:** CMRL publishes a matching tender reference, work description, contractor, award value, and award date for these entries.
- **Not established by this source:** payment or actual expenditure, physical completion, operating performance, project-level emissions reductions, population served, or distributional equity outcomes.
- **Climate and equity:** the source listing does not provide GHG calculations or measured access/equity outcomes. These fields are marked as not stated; no benefits are imputed from the work descriptions.
- **Amounts:** retain the value exactly as published and preserve its tax basis. Values with differing GST treatment should not be added together without normalization.
- **Dates:** retain the date text in the source's displayed format rather than infer a different locale.

This register complements the 23-row GCC Climate Budget crosswalk and the separate three-package drainage award cross-check. It does not modify or replace the supplied GCC starter CSV.

## How to reproduce the register

Open the CMRL source page and search for each tender reference in the CSV. The page may be updated over time; save a dated copy or page capture when a formal audit trail is required. The current CSV records the source URL but does not claim an immutable archived snapshot.
